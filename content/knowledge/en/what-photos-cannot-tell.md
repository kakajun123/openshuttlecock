A reading here is made from photos alone. That sets a clear limit on what it can say.

## What a photo can show

- **Feather shape.** How the tips are cut.
- **The shaft.** Whether it runs straight or bends, and how thick it is.
- **Consistency.** Whether all sixteen feathers match in height, spacing and colour.
- **Whiteness and marks.** Yellow or grey flecks, and uneven colour.
- **Visible defects.** Creases, broken shafts, split or missing parts of a feather.
- **Workmanship.** How even the glue is and how level the thread lines sit. This is listed separately and does not change the feather grade.

From these, the reading gives a grade tendency from 1 to 5, where 1 is the best.

## What a photo cannot show

- **The inside of the cork.** It cannot be judged without cutting it open.
- **Weight.** Small differences matter, and a photo has no scale.
- **Speed.** Speed is found by hitting the shuttle.
- **Flight stability.** It shows only in the air.
- **Durability.** It depends on storage, humidity and how the shuttle is played.

## What the reading will not say

It does not judge whether a shuttle is genuine, name the factory that made it, estimate a price or recommend a brand. It makes no promise about how a shuttle will play.

## How sure it is

Every line carries a confidence of high, medium or low. The reading uses words like "closer to" and "tends toward" because that is what a photo supports. An AI reading can be wrong. Treat it as a second opinion next to your own eyes and the seller's information.
