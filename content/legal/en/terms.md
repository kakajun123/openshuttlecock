## What a reading is

A reading is an AI model's description of the feathers it sees in your photos, with a grade tendency from 1 to 5 and a confidence level for each line.

- It is a tendency, not a verdict. It can be wrong.
- It does not judge whether a shuttlecock is genuine, and it does not name a maker.
- It does not estimate price or value.
- It says nothing about the inside of the cork, weight, speed, flight or durability.
- It is general information. It is not professional advice, a quality certificate, or evidence in a dispute.

Do not rely on a reading alone when you buy, sell, return or complain about a product.

## What you may upload

- Keep people out of the frame.
- Do not upload anything unlawful, offensive or unrelated.
- Do not try to trick or manipulate the AI, for example with text written in a photo or a question.
- Do not use scripts or other automated means to submit requests, and do not try to get around the daily limit.

You must be 18 or over, or have a parent's or guardian's consent.
